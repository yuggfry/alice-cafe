🔧 TECHNICAL DETAILS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SUBSCRIPTION PROCESS:

1. Customer submits form
2. POST /api/subscribers endpoint receives data
3. Email & name stored in database
4. Confirmation message returned
5. Success alert shown to customer

SENDING NEWSLETTER:

1. Owner fills form in dashboard
2. POST /api/newsletter/send endpoint
3. Backend:
   - Gets all active subscribers
   - Retrieves their email addresses
   - Sends HTML email to all (BCC)
   - Logs newsletter in history
4. Owner sees confirmation message
5. All emails sent within seconds

❓ COMMON QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Can customers unsubscribe?
A: Yes, tell them they can email you or you can manually deactivate.
   (Future: Auto-unsubscribe link can be added)

Q: What if I send to same list twice?
A: It's okay - they'll just get 2 emails. Check email addresses first.

Q: Can I schedule emails?
A: No, they send immediately. (Future: Scheduling can be added)

Q: How many subscribers can I have?
A: Unlimited! Gmail allows thousands.

Q: Do I see when they open emails?
A: Not yet. (Future: Analytics can be added)

Q: Can they reply to newsletter?
A: Depends on Gmail settings. They can email you directly.

Q: Are subscribers charged?
A: No! Newsletter is free for subscribers and to send.

Q: What if Gmail blocks my emails?
A: Check spam folder. Make sure app password is correct.
   Gmail might flag bulk emails (just tell people to add you to contacts).

Q: Can I send PDF/images in newsletter?
A: Yes! You can include images in the message text.

Q: How do I get more subscribers?
A: Promote the section on website, in cafe, on menus, social media.
