Our Story

Crafted with Passion

A Slice of Paris in Austin

Alice Poulain Café brings the charm and sophistication of Parisian café culture to Austin. Inspired by the classic French bistro tradition, our café celebrates artisanal craftsmanship in every cup and pastry.

Our carefully selected coffee beans are roasted locally by Texas Coffee Traders, featuring organic Latin American blends with a light body. For those seeking bolder flavors, we offer single-origin Indonesian coffee from Sumatra.

Every detail—from our vintage Parisian décor to our hand-crafted menu—reflects our commitment to creating an authentic, welcoming space where coffee lovers can gather, connect, and savor the moment.
