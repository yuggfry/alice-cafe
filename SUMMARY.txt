💼 HOW OWNER USES IT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SENDING OFFERS:
  1. Click "Owner Portal" on website
  2. Login with your email/password
  3. Click "Offers & Notices" tab
  4. Click "+ Add New Offer"
  5. Fill in title, description, date
  6. Click "Save" → Appears on website INSTANTLY!

SENDING NEWSLETTER:
  1. Go to "Newsletter" tab in dashboard
  2. Write subject line and message
  3. Click "Send to All Subscribers"
  4. All subscribers get beautiful email!

MANAGING RESERVATIONS:
  1. Go to "Reservations" tab
  2. See all booking requests
  3. Change status (Pending → Confirmed)
  4. View customer details

VIEWING SUBSCRIBERS:
  1. Go to "Subscribers" tab
  2. See everyone who subscribed
  3. Know who's interested in updates
